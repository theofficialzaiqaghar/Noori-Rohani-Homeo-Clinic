Questions

Common questions

Do you guarantee cure in Rohani Ilaj?
No. We offer ruqyah and guidance with sincerity. Healing is with Allah's will, and results differ for each person.
Can I come only for homeopathic treatment?
Yes. Book a homeo consultation and we will focus on your health history and symptoms.
Are herbal products safe to use with other medicine?
Tell us what you already take. We will advise if something should be avoided or spaced apart.
Is my visit kept private?
We do not share your details. Sensitive cases are discussed in a separate, quiet space when possible.
How do I apply for free school support?
Contact us with basic family information. Our team reviews cases as seats and funds allow.
What should I bring to the first visit?
Your ID, any previous reports, and a list of medicines you use. For Rohani cases, come with wudu if you can.
